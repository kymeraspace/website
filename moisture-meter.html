FAQ Humimeter

Precision Built
for Every Stage

◎

What does a leather moisture meter measure?

It measures the percentage of water content in leather — critical for quality control in tanning, drying and finishing stages.

◈

What production stages does the Humimeter LM6 cover?

The LM6 measures from 3% to 65% water content, covering the full production process from wet blue hides through to finished leather.

★

Is the measurement method destructive?

No. The LM6 uses a non-destructive method that does not damage or mark the leather surface in any way.

◉

To which standard is the device calibrated?

The LM6 is calibrated to the international reference method EN ISO 4684, ensuring accuracy and traceability.
