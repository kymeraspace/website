What we supply

Spare Parts for
Leather Splitting Machines

Kymera Group supplies original and compatible spare parts for leather splitting machines used in tanneries worldwide. We stock components compatible with the most widespread splitting machine brands — Mosconi, Alpespak, Rizzi, Linta, Gemata, Poletto, Mercier and Camoga.

Whether you need consumables like splitting blades and grinding wheels, or spare parts such as ring rollers, jaw plates or any other wear part, we source and supply the right component for your specific machine model. We work with leading consumables manufacturers including Lamebo, Alber and Norton — and every part is checked for dimensional accuracy before shipping, so you can order with confidence and minimise downtime on the production floor.

Quality Process

If It Doesn't Meet Spec,
It Doesn't Ship

Every spare part that leaves our warehouse is dimensionally checked against the original specification. The benchmark is simple: would we install this part in our own production line? If the answer is no, we don't send it to you.

  • Strict dimensional inspection on every batch — calipers and gauges, not visual checks
  • Sourced from leading consumables manufacturers (Lamebo, Alber, Norton)
  • Compatibility verified against your machine model and reference numbers
  • No minimum order quantities — single items or full kits
  • Fast dispatch from stock on the most common wear parts

Our spare parts are compatible with the most widespread brands, including Mosconi, Rizzi, Alpespak, Linta, Gemata, Poletto, Mercier and Camoga. Tell us your machine model and we will confirm the right part for you.

Do you supply original or compatible parts?

Both. We supply original parts from leading consumables manufacturers such as Lamebo, Alber and Norton, as well as high-quality compatible parts for wear items where compatible replacements deliver excellent performance at a better price.

How do you guarantee quality?

Every part is subjected to dimensional checks before dispatch. If a part doesn't meet specification, it doesn't leave our warehouse — so you order with confidence and minimise downtime on the production floor.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

No. We supply both original and compatible components with no minimum order quantities, so you can order a single replacement blade or a complete consumables package with the same care.

We stock the most common wear parts and consumables for fast dispatch. Lead times for less common parts depend on the item — contact us with your machine model and required part for a precise delivery estimate.

Yes. Send us your machine brand, model and a photo or reference number of the worn part — our team will identify the correct replacement and quote it for you.
