Instrument 02 — Softness

The ST300
Leather Softness Tester

The ST300 leather softness tester is a means of determining the softness of leather without defacing the hide or skin, and without cutting samples prior to testing. This enables a quality system to be set up between supplier and customer wherever leather softness is a priority. Available in analogue and digital versions, the ST300 was designed by MSA in conjunction with the BLC Leather Technology Centre.

The digital ST300 adds a USB interface for connection to a computer — with data download available via dedicated software and USB cable, or a wireless kit (module, receiver and software).

How It Works

From Hand-Feel
to Hard Number

◎

Clamp Over the Aperture

The leather is clamped over a defined ring aperture — no sample cutting, no marking. The finished or graded piece stays intact and saleable.

◈

Apply a Fixed Load

A pin presses down under a standardised load, pushing the leather through the aperture. The softer the leather, the further it distends.

★

Read the Distension

The instrument reads the distension in millimetres to ISO 17235 — analogue on a dial, or digital with a clear display and USB export.

◉

Record & Compare

Log the value against the batch. Supplier and customer work to one agreed softness target, replacing subjective judgement with repeatable data.

Questions & Answers

Softness Testing,
Explained

What is the ST300 softness tester used for?+
The ST300 determines the softness of leather to ISO 17235. It lets suppliers and customers agree a measurable softness specification and verify it on every batch — without cutting or defacing the hide.
How does the ST300 measure leather softness?+
Leather is clamped over a defined ring aperture and a pin applies a fixed load. The instrument reads the resulting distension (deflection) in millimetres — a higher value means softer leather. The test is fast, repeatable and non-destructive.
Is the ST300 test destructive?+
No. The ST300 measures softness without defacing the hide or skin, and does not require samples to be cut prior to testing — so graded and finished pieces remain fully saleable.
What is the difference between the analogue and digital ST300?+
Both versions perform the same ISO 17235 test. The analogue ST300 shows the reading on a dial, while the digital ST300 has a digital display and a USB interface for data download — via dedicated software and USB cable, or a wireless kit (module, receiver and software).
To which standard does the ST300 conform?+
The ST300 measures leather softness in accordance with ISO 17235. It was designed by MSA Engineering Systems in conjunction with the BLC Leather Technology Centre and is supplied by Kymera Group.
