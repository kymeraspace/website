Decision Guide

How to Choose the
Right Clip

Three quick questions to land on the right toggle for your production line.

Step 01

Check Your Frame Pin

Measure the hole diameter required by your toggling machine frame. Silver Toggle uses 23.5 mm; Eco and Black Toggle both use 24 mm — the two standard sizes across the industry.

Step 02

Match the Leather

Thin to medium chrome-tanned leathers grip perfectly with Silver or Eco. For thick hides, sole leather and vegetable tanned leather, the Black Toggle's 8.5 mm opening avoids slippage and damage.

Step 03

Pick Your Volume

FAQ Toggle Clips

Your Questions,
Answered

◎

What are tannery toggle clips used for?

Toggle clips are used on leather toggling machines to hold and stretch hides during the drying phase. They keep the leather under controlled tension, ensuring uniform drying, optimal yield and a stable, flat finished surface.

◈

What is the difference between Silver, Eco and Black Toggle?

Silver Toggle is a compact high-quality clip suitable for all leather types. Eco Toggle is a standard-dimension clip for general production. Black Toggle has a wider 8.5 mm opening and is specifically designed for thick and heavy hides and vegetable tanned leather.

★

Which toggle clip should I use for veg tan leather?

For vegetable tanned leather and heavy hides we recommend the Black Toggle, which has a larger 8.5 mm opening and a reinforced structure to grip thicker leathers without slipping.

◉

What materials are Kymera toggle clips made of?

Our toggle clips feature an aluminum body and a stainless steel pin for maximum corrosion resistance and lifespan. Specific variants are also produced in technical plastic for chemically aggressive environments.

↻

Are these clips compatible with my toggling machine?

Our clips fit the standard hole and pin dimensions used by all major tannery toggling machine manufacturers. Send us your machine brand and model and we will confirm compatibility before quoting.

◆

Can I order custom quantities?

Yes. We supply toggle clips in any quantity, from small replacement orders to full machine kits and bulk industrial orders. Contact us with your required quantity for a tailored quote.
