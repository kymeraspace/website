What we supply

Spare Parts for
Leather Wringers

Kymera Group supplies spare parts and consumables for the wringer — the machine tanneries call the sammying machine, or wet press (WB press, pressa bagnato). It removes excess water from wet hides after the wet-tanning operations: the hide passes through the nip between two felt-covered rollers under hydraulic pressure, the felts absorb the water squeezed out, and the hide leaves much drier, smoothed and partly stretched — ready for setting out, splitting or shaving.

From the consumables that touch the hide every cycle — the felt sleeves and the introduction belt — through to the roller rubber, the doctor blades that keep the felts clean and the seal kits that keep the hydraulic system holding pressure, we source the right component for your specific machine. Tell us your brand, model and roller size, and we'll match the part, confirm the specification and quote it for you.

How it works

Wringing the Hide, Step by Step

In the wet end, the wringer (wet press) squeezes the water out of the hide between felt-covered rollers. The hide is fed in flat, pressed through the nip and the water is drawn into the felts — here is the cycle on the production floor.

01

Feed the Hide

The wet hide is laid flat on the introduction belt and carried square and creaseless into the nip between the felt-covered rollers.

02

Press the Nip

The hydraulic system closes the rollers and the hide passes through the nip under high pressure across its full width.

03

Wring & Smooth

The felts absorb the water squeezed out of the hide; it leaves much drier, with the grain smoothed and partly stretched.

04

Clean & Repeat

Doctor blades and the washing system clean and re-wet the felts so they keep absorbing, hide after hide, without marking the grain.

Your Questions,

What is a wringer (sammying machine) used for in a tannery?

A wringer — also called a sammying machine or wet press (WB press) — removes excess water from wet hides after the wet-tanning operations. The hide passes through the nip between two felt-covered rollers under hydraulic pressure; the felts absorb the water squeezed out, so the hide leaves much drier, smoothed and partly stretched, ready for setting out, splitting or shaving.

◈

What are the felts on a wringer for?

The felt sleeves wrap the rollers and do the actual de-watering: as the wet hide goes through the press the felts absorb the water squeezed out of it. They are the main wear item — their condition determines how much moisture the press removes and how smooth and creaseless the grain comes out, so they are replaced periodically.

★

What is the introduction belt on a wringer?

The introduction (feeding) belt carries the wet hide flat into the nip between the felt-covered rollers. It keeps the hide square and creaseless as it enters the press, so the wringing is even across the full width. It is a wear part, replaced when it stretches, tracks off or marks the leather.

◉

Can the wringer rollers be re-rubberised?

Yes. We supply rubber re-covering for the wringer rollers and new rubber-covered rollers, restoring the correct hardness and grip so the press wrings evenly across the whole width. Tell us the roller diameter, length and brand and we will match the covering.

↻

Which wringer / sammying machine brands do your spare parts fit?

Our parts and consumables are compatible with the most widespread brands, including Escomar, Bauce, CM, Rizzi and Mercier. Tell us your machine model and we will confirm the correct part for you.

◆

What is the difference between a wringer and a setting-out machine?

A wringer (wet press) removes water from the hide by squeezing it between felt-covered rollers; a setting-out machine then stretches and smooths the de-watered hide with a bladed roller to flatten it and remove growth wrinkles. They are consecutive steps — the wringer takes the water out, setting out opens the hide up.
