Process

How Does
It Work?

01
◎

Place the Hide

Position the leather side onto the machine with easy commands and regulations. The process is simple and ergonomic.

02
◈

Inflate & Stretch

The machine stretches the leather in all directions — like a balloon — revealing the true surface and structure of the hide.

03
◉

Inspect Defects

All micro-defects and surface imperfections that would otherwise pass undetected are now clearly visible and easy to mark.

04
★

Mark & Decide

Mark defective areas instantly and decide which pieces proceed. With the CP model, check cut pieces with much less effort.
