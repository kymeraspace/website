FAQ

Buffing paper: frequent questions

Which buffing paper grit should I use for nubuck?

Nubuck is usually buffed with fine grits, typically P320–P400 with KP936E, or finer with KP903E up to P1500 when you need a very short, uniform nap. Send us your article and the result you want and we confirm the grit before you order.

What is the difference between KP936E and KP903E?

KP936E has a double resin bond for strong cutting and even removal, standard grits P80–P400: the general-purpose paper for splits, suede and corrected grain. KP903E has a glue-over-glue bond with an anti-clogging top coat and goes up to P1500: for fine buffing of leather and for sanding finishes and fillers without the paper loading up.

In which formats is the buffing paper supplied?

Both papers come in 610 mm wide rolls or as belts. For belts, tell us your machine model and belt size.

Are non-standard grits available?

Yes. For KP936E, grits P40–P60 and P500–P1000 are produced on request with a minimum order quantity.
